The problem it solves / किस परेशानी का हल

😩 Today / अभी

  • SBI gives each CSP a long printed list of inoperative accounts - लंबी सूची
  • The CSP has to call every customer one by one - एक-एक को फ़ोन
  • Most never hear about it, accounts stay closed - खाते बंद ही रहते हैं

😊 With Grahak Sampark / अब

  • Take one photo of the list - बस एक फोटो
  • The agent reads every name, mobile and account - सब अपने आप पढ़ा जाता है
  • Each customer gets a WhatsApp message in Hindi + English - हर ग्राहक को संदेश

4 simple steps / 4 आसान कदम

1

Photo / फोटो

CSP takes a photo of the list with the phone, or picks one from the gallery.

2

Agent reads / पढ़ता है

Reads the list twice, straightens tilted photos, checks every mobile and account number.

3

CSP checks / जाँच

Anything unclear is shown in red. CSP matches with the paper and saves.

4

WhatsApp / संदेश

One tap per customer - WhatsApp opens with the message ready. Customers come with KYC.

What the customer receives / ग्राहक को क्या मिलता है

  • Message in Hindi + English - हिंदी और अंग्रेज़ी
  • Only the first name and last 4 digits of the account - पूरा नंबर कभी नहीं
  • The CSP's shop name and address - where to come - कहाँ आना है
  • What to bring: Aadhaar / PAN - क्या लाना है
  • Reply STOP and they are never messaged again - STOP पर बंद

Safe by design / पूरी तरह सुरक्षित

🔒
Offline readingThe photo is read on Eko's own server - never sent to any outside company.
🗑️
List stays on the CSP's phoneThe photo is read and forgotten - no list is ever stored on the server; deleted from the phone after {{ retention_days }} days (DPDP Act).
🎯
Minimum dataOnly name, mobile and account are kept; father's name, CIF, address are dropped.
🔁
Read twiceTwo independent readings - any number that doesn't match is shown in red.
✋
CSP has the final sayNothing is sent until the CSP checks the list against the paper.
👤
Own login for every CSPEach CSP sees only their own lists. Every action is recorded.

Who uses it / कौन इस्तेमाल करता है

🏪 CSP

Opens the link on the phone, logs in with the CSP code, uploads the list photo, checks it and sends the messages from WhatsApp. Nothing to install.

👑 Super admin (Eko)

Creates CSP logins, resets passwords, switches CSPs on or off, and sees totals and recent activity for all CSPs.

Tested on real SBI lists

On the two sample lists, the agent read 102 of 102 customers correctly - including a curled phone photo.
