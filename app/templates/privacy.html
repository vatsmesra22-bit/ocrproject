Privacy notice / गोपनीयता सूचना

How Grahak Sampark uses the data of SBI customers whose accounts have become inoperative, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who / कौन

Grahak Sampark is run by Eko Bharat Ventures for SBI Customer Service Points (CSPs). Your bank (SBI) shares the list of inoperative accounts with its CSP so that you can be told how to reactivate your account.

What data / कौन सा डेटा

Only your name, mobile number and account number. Other details on the bank's list (father's name, CIF, address) are not kept. The photo of the list is deleted as soon as it is read.

Why / क्यों

Only to send you one message: that your SBI account is inoperative and how to reactivate it at your CSP. It is not used for anything else, not sold and not shared with anyone else.

How long / कितने दिन

Lists are never stored on Eko's server - they stay only on the CSP's phone and are deleted from it after {{ retention_days }} days. A record of actions (without your name or full number) is kept for one year.

Your rights / आपके अधिकार

  • Stop messages: reply STOP to the message - you will not be messaged again.
  • Know, correct or delete your data: contact us below; we will erase it from every list.
  • Complain: contact us below. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
